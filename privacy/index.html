Qatra Dunes — Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Qatra Dunes can be played offline. Optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob and optional coin purchases use Apple’s App Store. These services connect to the internet and process information as described below. Creating a Qatra account is optional. There is no developer-operated analytics server.

What the app keeps on your device

The game saves your scores, discovered worlds, tutorial progress, sound/haptic settings, Rival Lines ghost runs, expedition achievements, gold balance, owned boards and upgrades. It also saves purchase transaction identifiers so the same purchase cannot add gold twice. If you sign in to a Qatra account, the sign-in is kept in your iPhone’s Keychain. This gameplay wallet is stored locally and is not synchronized between devices. Deleting the saved app data can remove your wallet and collection; consumable coin packs cannot be restored after that deletion.

Optional Qatra account

You can play without an account. If you choose to create a Qatra account (or sign in with one), the same account works in Qatra Dunes and in Qatra, the browser game. The Qatra server keeps your email address, a securely hashed form of your password (never the password itself), and the Qatra character the account comes with. Your Dunes gold, boards and progress stay on your device and are not sent to the server.

The email address is used to sign you in and, only when you ask, to send a password-reset code. Reset emails are delivered through our email provider, Resend. We don't send marketing email and don't share your address with advertisers.

You can delete the account at any time in the game (menu → Qatra account → Delete account). That permanently removes the account, its email address, its sign-ins and its Qatra character from the server.

Rewarded advertising

Watching an ad is optional. Completing an AdMob rewarded ad returns one heart to a finished Endless run, once per run. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address (including approximate location), device identifiers, ad interactions, product interactions and diagnostic/performance information to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request access to the iOS advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing by Google.

Google’s User Messaging Platform gathers consent where required before an ad is requested. When available, Ad privacy choices in the game menu lets you revisit your choices. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Optional purchases

Apple handles payment, purchase confirmation and transaction verification. The game receives the product and transaction information needed to deliver gold; it does not receive your payment-card details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Device access

The game does not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or health data. There is no chat or user-generated content. Purchases respect Apple’s purchase-approval and family controls, including Ask to Buy.
