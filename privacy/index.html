Sand Surge — Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Sand Surge can be played offline. Rewarded and interstitial ads use Google AdMob and optional coin purchases use Apple’s App Store. These services connect to the internet and process information as described below. Creating a Sand Surge account is optional. There is no developer-operated analytics server.

What the app keeps on your device

The game saves your scores, discovered worlds, tutorial progress, sound/haptic settings, Rival Lines ghost runs, expedition achievements, gold balance, owned boards and upgrades. It also saves purchase transaction identifiers so the same purchase cannot add gold twice. If you sign in to a Sand Surge account, the sign-in is kept in your iPhone’s Keychain. Played signed out, this gameplay wallet is stored only on the device; deleting the saved app data can remove it, and consumable coin packs cannot be restored after that deletion. Signed in, the game also keeps a copy for that account on the device and saves it to your Sand Surge account (below).

The App Clip

The Sand Surge App Clip is a one-minute demo ride. It shows no ads, offers no account or purchases, and collects nothing. It keeps your best distance on your iPhone, where the full game picks it up if you install it; it never leaves the device.

Optional Sand Surge account

You can play without an account. If you choose to create a Sand Surge account (or sign in with one), the same account also works in Qatra, the browser game. The account server keeps your username, email address, a securely hashed form of your password if you choose password authentication (never the password itself), and the Qatra character the account comes with. While you are signed in, the server also keeps your Sand Surge progress so it is there when you sign in on another iPhone: your gold balance and the gold changes that make it up, the App Store transactions of coin packs (to credit each pack once), owned boards, upgrades and finishes, your career, personal bests, trail medals, chosen rider and Explore progress. The first time you sign in, progress made on that iPhone while signed out moves to the account. Sound and haptic settings and the free-gold ad allowance stay on the device. While signed in, the app submits your best distance and stores your rider name for the global top 50 and friend leaderboards, even if you do not open Friends. Your username is the initial rider name shown to other players; you can change that display name on Friends. Friends can see your best distance and the date of your most recent run. Opening Friends or syncing a record does not count as playing. Friend challenges store the participants, shared course, attempt start/finish times and distances; each result is visible to those two players. Unsent challenge results stay on the phone until they can sync. Deleting an account also removes its activity and challenges. Username availability is checked with the server while you type during signup.

You can also sign in or sign up with Apple. Apple verifies your identity; we receive an identifier for your Apple account and your verified email address (including an Apple private-relay address if you choose Hide My Email). We do not request your real name from Apple. New players choose a public username before registration finishes. We exchange a one-use authorization code with Apple and store an encrypted Apple refresh token to manage the connection and revoke it when you delete your account. Incomplete Apple registrations expire after 20 minutes. Existing password accounts can connect Apple from account settings after signing in; matching email addresses alone do not link accounts. You can add a password for the browser game through the password-reset flow using the email shown in account settings.

Usernames and rider names are user-generated content. Your rider name, best distance and Premium badge can appear on the global leaderboard. Friends also see your recent activity and challenge results. We store friend connections, invite codes and their expiry times, blocks, and reports (reporter, reported account, name snapshot, reason, review status and timestamps) to run and moderate these features. Public names are filtered; offensive names or impersonation can be reported from player controls. You can block a player, remove a friend, or replace your invite code in Friends. Blocks hide the two players from one another’s social results, prevent new friend connections and challenges, and stop social push alerts between them.

If you enable notifications, we send your Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token and delivery environment to the account server. The token is linked to your account to deliver friend and challenge alerts via Apple, which can contain a rider name and score. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings.

The email address is used to sign you in and, only when you ask, to send a password-reset code. Reset emails are delivered through our email provider, Resend. We don’t send marketing email and don’t share your address with advertisers.

You can delete the account at any time in the game (menu → Sand Surge account → Delete account). That permanently removes the account, its username, its email address, its sign-ins, its Qatra character and its Sand Surge progress from the server. For a connected Apple account, deletion also queues revocation of Apple’s token. Only the encrypted token and Apple identifier remain in that retry queue until Apple confirms revocation, after which they are removed. This lets account deletion finish even if Apple is temporarily unavailable.

Advertising

Rewarded ads play when you choose a reward. For players without Premium, interstitial ads may also appear automatically between runs, subject to the app’s frequency limits; they are not limited to ads you request. Premium removes ads. Completing an AdMob rewarded ad can return one heart to a finished Endless run (once per run), double the gold that run collected (once per run), or add free gold in the workshop (a few times a day). Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address (including approximate location), device identifiers, ad interactions, product interactions and diagnostic/performance information to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request access to the iOS advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing by Google.

Google’s User Messaging Platform gathers consent where required before an ad is requested. When available, Ad privacy choices in the game menu lets you revisit your choices. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Optional purchases

Apple handles payment, purchase confirmation and transaction verification. The game receives the product and transaction information needed to deliver gold, or to know that you own Sand Surge Premium (it notes that on your device so the game stays ad-free offline). If you are signed in, the app sends signed App Store transaction data (JWS), product and transaction identifiers and the account’s purchase token to our server for coin-pack and Premium verification, fraud prevention and duplicate-credit protection. The server records purchase history and entitlements so your purchases and gold can follow that account to another iPhone. The game does not receive your payment-card details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Device access

The game does not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or health data. There is no free-text chat. Public usernames and rider names are user-generated content. Purchases respect Apple’s purchase-approval and family controls, including Ask to Buy.

Retention and safety

Account-linked saves, purchases, social connections, notification tokens and safety records are kept while the account exists and removed when you delete it. Reports can be reviewed by the service operator to remove names or suspend social access. Emailing support sends us the address and information you include. Contact contact@qatras.com for privacy or safety concerns, including a name-filter appeal. Please do not put your real name or contact details in a public rider name.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change. Questions: contact@qatras.com
