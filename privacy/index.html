SandSurge — Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

SandSurge can be played offline. Optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob and optional coin purchases use Apple’s App Store. These services connect to the internet and process information as described below. Creating a SandSurge account is optional. There is no developer-operated analytics server.

What the app keeps on your device

The game saves your scores, discovered worlds, tutorial progress, sound/haptic settings, Rival Lines ghost runs, expedition achievements, gold balance, owned boards and upgrades. It also saves purchase transaction identifiers so the same purchase cannot add gold twice. If you sign in to a SandSurge account, the sign-in is kept in your iPhone’s Keychain. Played signed out, this gameplay wallet is stored only on the device; deleting the saved app data can remove it, and consumable coin packs cannot be restored after that deletion. Signed in, the game also keeps a copy for that account on the device and saves it to your SandSurge account (below).

The App Clip

The SandSurge App Clip is a one-minute demo ride. It shows no ads, offers no account or purchases, and collects nothing. It keeps your best distance on your iPhone, where the full game picks it up if you install it; it never leaves the device.

Optional SandSurge account

You can play without an account. If you choose to create a SandSurge account (or sign in with one), the same account also works in Qatra, the browser game. The account server keeps your username, email address, a securely hashed form of your password (never the password itself), and the Qatra character the account comes with. While you are signed in, the server also keeps your SandSurge progress so it is there when you sign in on another iPhone: your gold balance and the gold changes that make it up, the App Store transactions of coin packs (to credit each pack once), owned boards, upgrades and finishes, your career, personal bests, trail medals, chosen rider and Explore progress. The first time you sign in, progress made on that iPhone while signed out moves to the account. Sound and haptic settings and the free-gold ad allowance stay on the device. If you use the leaderboard, the server also stores your rider name and best distance. Your username is the initial rider name shown to other players; you can change that display name on the leaderboard. Username availability is checked with the server while you type during signup.

The email address is used to sign you in and, only when you ask, to send a password-reset code. Reset emails are delivered through our email provider, Resend. We don’t send marketing email and don’t share your address with advertisers.

You can delete the account at any time in the game (menu → SandSurge account → Delete account). That permanently removes the account, its username, its email address, its sign-ins, its Qatra character and its SandSurge progress from the server.

Rewarded advertising

Watching an ad is optional, and an ad only plays when you choose one. Completing an AdMob rewarded ad can return one heart to a finished Endless run (once per run), double the gold that run collected (once per run), or add free gold in the workshop (a few times a day). Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address (including approximate location), device identifiers, ad interactions, product interactions and diagnostic/performance information to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request access to the iOS advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing by Google.

Google’s User Messaging Platform gathers consent where required before an ad is requested. When available, Ad privacy choices in the game menu lets you revisit your choices. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Optional purchases

Apple handles payment, purchase confirmation and transaction verification. The game receives the product and transaction information needed to deliver gold, or to know that you own SandSurge Premium (it notes that on your device so the game stays ad-free offline). If you are signed in, the account server verifies your Premium purchase with Apple and records it on your account, so Premium is on for that account on any iPhone. The game does not receive your payment-card details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Device access

The game does not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or health data. There is no chat or user-generated content. Purchases respect Apple’s purchase-approval and family controls, including Ask to Buy.

Changes and contact
