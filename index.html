Qatra Dunes

Surf the dunes, outrun the worm. An endless sand-surfing game for iPhone.

Support

Questions, a problem with the game, or a purchase that didn't arrive? Email contact@qatras.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I play? Swipe left or right to change lane, up to jump and down to duck, or use the on-screen buttons. Collect coins and power-ups and keep ahead of the sandworm.

I bought gold but it isn't showing. Purchases are confirmed by the App Store and added as soon as it approves them; an Ask to Buy purchase arrives when it's approved. Reopen the Board Workshop with an internet connection. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date of the purchase.

Can I move my gold to another device? Not at the moment: your progress and gold are stored on your device.

What is a Qatra account? An optional sign-in shared with Qatra, the browser game: one email and password for both. Create one or sign in from the game's menu under Qatra account. Forgot your password? Choose "Forgot your password?" there and we'll email you a code.

How do I delete my account? In the game, open the menu, choose Qatra account, then Delete account and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes the account and its Qatra character. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on the account and we'll delete it for you.

How do I turn off sound or haptics? Use the switches on the start or pause screen.

Ads. Ads are optional: watch one after a run to return with one heart. You can review your ad privacy choices from the game's menu.
